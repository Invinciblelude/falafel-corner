## Session 2: Three Demographic Groups + Medical Pitch

### Group A: Health-Conscious "Biohackers" & Gym Rats
- *Pain:* Want high protein, bored of Chipotle, tired of cooking
- *Support:* Pay $15–18 for Protocol Bowl; "investment-minded" about bodies
- *Targets:* California Family Fitness, 24 Hour Fitness, CrossFit/MMA on Marconi & Watt

### Group B: "Medical Row" Professionals
- *Pain:* Pharma reps/office managers need lunch for 20+; corporate budget, not personal
- *Support:* Reliability, Halal, labeling; "Food as Medicine" angle
- *Targets:* Kaiser Morse Ave, Mercy San Juan, dental/specialty clinics, Sacramento Heart & Vascular, Quest Diagnostics

### Group C: Middle Eastern & Slavic (Value Seekers)
- *Truth:* Not cheap — value-driven; know what good Mediterranean costs at home
- *Strategy:* Don't sell individual bowls. Sell **Family Fuel Packs**
- *Pitch:* "Feed 6 people for $65" — value-per-pound, volume margins

### Extended Hit List
| Category | Targets | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| Gyms | California Family Fitness, Results Transformation, MMA/Boxing | Recovery bowls post-workout |
| Medical | Kaiser Morse, Sacramento Heart & Vascular, Quest Diagnostics | Staff lunches, pharma rep catering |
| Schools | Mira Loma, El Camino, Arcade Fundamental Middle | Teacher workdays, sports pre-game (halal) |
| Auto | Fulton Ave Luxury (Lexus, BMW, Niello) | Saturday sales-team lunches |

### Value Framing (Avoid "Cheap" Objection)
- Don't say: "$16 for a bowl"
- Do say: "The $160 Weekly Office Protocol" (10 meals, Mon delivery)
- Do say: "The $75 Family Feast" (bulk protein, rice, salad)
- Move from **transactional** → **contractual** (health result, convenience)

### Medical Office 30-Second Pitch
> *"You've probably done Subway or pizza for staff appreciation—cheap, but carbs and sugar. Staff crash at 2pm. We bring Mediterranean: grilled chicken, falafel, salads, hummus, all halal, labeled. One order works for everyone. Hospitals are moving to food-as-medicine—our stuff fits. ~$15/person. First order 20% off. Can I leave a sample and a one-pager?"*

- Avoid: "too medical" or "too expensive"

We took the Mediterranean flavors you already love—flame-grilled chicken, creamy hummus, fresh-baked pita—and mapped them to PeptideEats Protocols. Same price as a standard combo, but engineered to keep your energy steady all day. You're not just buying a meal; you're buying a better afternoon."*

### Gym & Fitness — Protocol Guide (Fridge Cheat Sheet)

| Goal | "Fuel" Selection | Why It Works |
|------|------------------|--------------|
| **Muscle Gain** | Performance Platter (Double Chicken, Rice, Hummus) | High-leucine protein for MPS + complex carbs for glycogen |

| **Quick Fuel** | Balance Wrap (Chicken Shawarma, Wheat Wrap, Veggies) | Portable, balanced macros, on-the-go |

### Dual Signal Strategy
- **Halal** = trust signal (quality, premium meat) for Middle Eastern + health-conscious
- **Peptide** = premium signal (science behind menu) for gym + medical

### Taste Test Blitz Concept

- Makes gym-goers feel part of an "exclusive club"
