## 5. Strategic Focus Given Demographics

### Do *not* lean on

1. **Middle Eastern walk-in in 95821** — 0.25% Arab, lots of cheap options nearby.  
2. **Student meal contracts** — slow, bureaucratic, low margins.  
3. **Price competition** — you can’t win vs $8 falafel wraps on retail.

### Do focus on

1. **B2B catering** — institutions pay $12–18/person; your pricing fits.  
2. **Offices + medical** — Arden Way/Kaiser/Morse corridor, stable employer base.  
3. **Gyms** — 95821 has CrossFit New Heights; health/protein angle is differentiated.  
4. **Islamic community** — SALAM Arden (985 Enterprise), events, iftar; halal is a requirement, not a nice-to-have.  
5. **Higher Arab % zips (95841, 95864)** — as delivery/event radius expands, not as primary retail focus.
